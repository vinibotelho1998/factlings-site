Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

Factlings Studio connects only to the Factlings accounts, with the permission of the account owner, to upload Factlings videos and read basic account information such as the account name and public counts.

What we store: the access tokens the platforms issue for our own accounts, kept only on our own computer, in files readable only by the owner. We do not store passwords.

What we do not do: we do not collect data about viewers or other users, we do not sell or share data, and we do not use any data for advertising.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform, and the stored tokens are deleted from our computer when a connection is removed.
